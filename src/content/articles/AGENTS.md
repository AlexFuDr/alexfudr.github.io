# Article context

For article research, drafting, translation, or medical-claim review, read `skills/acupuncture-evidence-editor/SKILL.md` from the repository root. From that skill, load only the routed references needed for the current task:

- evidence search or updates: `references/evidence-workflow.md`;
- writing, editing, or translation: `references/editorial-standard.md`;
- front matter, paired URLs, or publication checks: `references/content-contract.md`.

Non-negotiable rules:

- never publish machine-generated medical text without source and human review;
- keep English and Dutch conclusions, numbers, uncertainty, and safety statements aligned;
- preserve a shared `translation_key` and reciprocal `alternate_path` for each pair;
- do not provide patient-specific diagnosis or treatment advice.
