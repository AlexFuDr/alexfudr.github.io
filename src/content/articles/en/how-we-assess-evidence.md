This website does not begin with the broad question “Does acupuncture work?” We begin with a more precise one: **for whom, for which outcome, compared with what, and with how much certainty?**

## We search systematically

For each clinical topic, we first define a focused research question. We then run reproducible searches for guidelines, systematic reviews, and relevant primary studies. We retain the search date, strategy, and source set so that the assessment can be updated later.

## Not every study answers the same question

A comparison with sham acupuncture asks something different from a comparison with usual care or no treatment. A change in a laboratory marker is not automatically a benefit that a patient can feel. We therefore describe the population, comparator, and outcome behind each conclusion.

## Uncertainty belongs in the answer

We look beyond whether a result is positive or negative. We consider study design, size, risk of bias, variation between studies, clinical importance, adverse events, and conflicts of interest. Conflicting findings remain visible.

## Nordon assists; the source decides

Nordon helps us run PubMed search strategies, organize literature, and prepare provisional summaries. Important claims and numbers are then checked manually against the original publication or guideline. Automatically generated text is not published directly.

## What to expect from our articles

A substantive article explains what the strongest evidence indicates, what remains uncertain, which safety information matters, and when professional medical assessment is important. This website provides education and does not replace personal diagnosis or treatment.
