# Editorial standard

## Reader promise

Answer the reader's question early, then show why the answer is trustworthy. A page should help someone make a better-informed decision without pretending to replace a clinician.

## Recommended article shape

Use only sections that serve the topic, normally:

1. short answer;
2. what the intervention or question means;
3. what the strongest evidence shows;
4. what remains uncertain;
5. safety and situations requiring professional care;
6. practical interpretation;
7. author, review date, and references.

Do not force every article into an identical outline. Mechanism and history pages need different emphasis from condition pages.

## Claims language

Match wording to evidence:

- Higher/moderate certainty: “Evidence indicates…” or “Acupuncture probably…”
- Low certainty: “Acupuncture may…” or “The evidence suggests, but does not establish…”
- Very low or conflicting certainty: “It is uncertain whether…”
- Mechanism only: “This has been proposed as a mechanism…”

Avoid `proves`, `cures`, `detoxifies`, `balances hormones`, `boosts immunity`, or similar claims unless the precise, measurable meaning and adequate evidence are supplied. Never convert a surrogate, laboratory marker, animal result, or imaging association into a patient benefit.

## Safety

Include material adverse events, contraindications, practitioner/sterility context, and red-flag guidance appropriate to the topic. Keep safety proportional: do not imply that “natural” means safe, and do not exaggerate rare harms.

## Bilingual editorial policy

- Maintain one scientific conclusion across English and Dutch.
- Localize terminology and examples; do not translate word-for-word when that harms clarity.
- Use established Dutch healthcare terminology and natural Dutch prose.
- Preserve effect estimates, units, citations, cautions, and certainty labels exactly across languages.
- Human-review both versions before marking the pair complete.
- If only one version is ready, publish only when the site's untranslated-state behavior is implemented; never create a thin placeholder translation.

## Final review

Check factual accuracy, source proximity, spelling, accessible headings, descriptive links, image rights/alt text, conflicts of interest, and consistency between title, summary, structured data, and body.
